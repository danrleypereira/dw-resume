# Intro Video Script (1–3 minutes)

*Target: ~2.5 minutes at a natural, conversational pace (~330 words). Phone camera is fine. Speak it, don't read it — the notes below are a guide, not a teleprompter. Look at the lens, smile at the start and end.*

---

**[Open — friendly, relaxed]**

Hi, I'm Danrley. I'm a software engineer based in Brasília, Brazil, and I've been building software professionally since 2017.

**[Background — the arc]**

Most of my career has been full-stack and end-to-end: I like owning a product from the idea and the data model all the way to the interface, the tests, and the infrastructure it runs on. I've done that across startups, a large enterprise, and government.

The turning point for me was a project at Epicor, where I delivered an AI-powered feature end to end, connected to their real ERP, and shipped it to actual customers in a little over a year. After that, I decided to bet on myself and focus on my own company, DW Corp.

**[What I've been building — founder mindset]**

What I've really been doing is building the blocks that let me start a product fast without giving up quality: my own authentication service, payment integrations, a bare-metal cluster with full monitoring, CI/CD, infrastructure as code. Along the way I hired and led a small team, mentored more than ten engineers, and I lead the first Software Craftsmanship community here in Brazil, where we do TDD katas and round tables.

**[Why this role]**

Now that most of those blocks are in place, I'm looking for the right product to pour that into — and this role fits really well. It's the stack I work in every day: Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, real-time systems, Next.js. And the domain clicks with me: I've built procurement and ERP software, transport and logistics systems, and I have an MBA in Supply Chain, so operational software where data drives real-world work is something I genuinely enjoy.

**[Close — warm, direct]**

I'm looking for a founder to partner with closely, take ownership, and build something that delivers real value. I'm available full-time, I'd love to work hybrid or on-site, and I'm happy to travel. Thanks for watching — I'd really enjoy talking more.
