\cvsection{Softskills}

\begin{description}
    \item[Teamwork:] Adept at collaborating with others to achieve common goals.
    \item[Self-taught:] Eager to learn and able to acquire new skills independently.
    \item[Critical Thinking:] Skilled at analyzing situations and making informed decisions.
    \item[Innovative:] Constantly seeking new approaches and solutions to challenges.
    \item[Organized:] Efficient in managing tasks, time, and resources.
    \item[Agile:] Adaptable to changing environments and quick to adjust to new requirements.
    \item[Observer:] Pays attention to details, ensuring accuracy and quality in work.
    \item[Responsible:] Reliable and accountable in fulfilling duties.
    \item[Collaboration:] Enthusiastic about working with others to produce better outcomes.
    \item[Communication:] Effective in conveying ideas clearly and understanding others.
    \item[Trust:] Upholds integrity and earns trust from colleagues and superiors.
    \item[Self-Management:] Capable of working autonomously and managing one's own tasks.
    \item[Focus:] Undeterred concentration on the task at hand, ensuring timely completion.
    \item[Enthusiasm:] Passionate about work and always eager to take on new challenges.
    \item[Empathy:] Understands and respects the emotions and needs of others.
\end{description}
