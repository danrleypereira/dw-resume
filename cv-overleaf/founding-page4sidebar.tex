\cvsection{Soft Skills}

\begin{description}
    \item[Ownership:] Takes end-to-end responsibility — challenges ideas, proposes better solutions, and drives them to production rather than executing tickets.
    \item[Founder Mindset:] Comfortable building from zero, wearing multiple hats, and moving fast without breaking reliability.
    \item[Communication:] Clear, logical, and proactive collaborator; works closely with founders and non-technical stakeholders.
    \item[Mentorship:] Grows engineers and sets the standards a team inherits.
    \item[Critical Thinking:] Analyzes trade-offs and makes decisive, well-reasoned technical calls.
    \item[Self-Management:] Organized and autonomous; reliable under ambiguity and early-stage pressure.
\end{description}
